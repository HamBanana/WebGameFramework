### Resource Types in Acca

This document provides a detailed overview of the resource types in **Acca**, their purposes, sources, and gameplay integration.

---

### Overview of Resource Types

Resources in **Acca** serve as foundational elements for the game's economy and strategic mechanics. Players must acquire, manage, and trade resources to build businesses, maintain properties, and control regions. Each resource type has unique interactions within the game’s ecosystem.

---

### Resource Descriptions

#### 1. **Wood**

- **Purpose**: Used for constructing basic structures, small businesses, and furniture production.
- **Sources**:
  - Harvested from forest cells on the grid.
  - Purchased from markets or traded with other players.
  - Acquired through chance events (e.g., "Resource Boom").
- **Gameplay Integration**: Wood is an essential resource for early-game development and acts as a tradable commodity.

#### 2. **Steel**

- **Purpose**: Required for constructing factories, infrastructure, and advanced buildings.
- **Sources**:
  - Produced by steel mills owned by players.
  - Purchased from markets or obtained through trade.
  - Gained through chance events (e.g., "Industrial Surge").
- **Gameplay Integration**: Steel enables mid-to-late game expansion and is crucial for large-scale projects.

#### 3. **Electricity**

- **Purpose**: Powers businesses, amenities, and advanced infrastructure.
- **Sources**:
  - Generated by owning power plants.
  - Purchased from energy markets.
  - Obtained through random events (e.g., "Energy Surplus").
- **Gameplay Integration**: Electricity introduces a recurring upkeep cost, requiring strategic resource planning.

#### 4. **Water**

- **Purpose**: Sustains populations and is required for agricultural businesses and regional amenities.
- **Sources**:
  - Extracted from wells or reservoirs.
  - Purchased from markets.
  - Gained through events like "Rainy Season."
- **Gameplay Integration**: Water influences population happiness and productivity, affecting labor availability.

#### 5. **Food**

- **Purpose**: Supports population growth and sustains regional labor forces.
- **Sources**:
  - Produced by farms and food-processing businesses.
  - Purchased or traded with players.
  - Acquired through events like "Bumper Harvest."
- **Gameplay Integration**: Food is essential for population management, directly impacting happiness and migration.

#### 6. **Coal**

- **Purpose**: Fuels older industries and serves as a transitional energy resource.
- **Sources**:
  - Extracted from coal mines.
  - Purchased or traded in markets.
  - Obtained through events (e.g., "Coal Reserve Discovery").
- **Gameplay Integration**: Coal supports early industrialization but may be phased out for electricity in the late game.

#### 7. **Oil**

- **Purpose**: Powers advanced factories and transportation systems, enabling population migration mechanics.
- **Sources**:
  - Extracted from oil rigs in resource-rich regions.
  - Purchased from global markets.
  - Acquired through chance events (e.g., "Oil Discovery").
- **Gameplay Integration**: Oil is a high-value resource, vital for advanced industries and strategic trading.

---

### Resource Mechanics

#### Market Dynamics

- **Fluctuating Prices**: Resource prices change based on supply and demand, creating opportunities for strategic buying and selling.

#### Regional Specialization

- Each region specializes in certain resources, encouraging trade and strategic control of key areas.

#### Resource Combinations

- Advanced businesses require combinations of resources (e.g., steel + electricity for factories).

#### Infinite Storage

- Players have infinite storage capacity for resources, allowing them to focus solely on resource acquisition and management without storage limitations.

---

### Integration with Gameplay

- **Trading**: Players can trade resources to balance shortages or gain strategic advantages.
- **Chance Events**: Random events impact resource availability, adding unpredictability.
- **Population Happiness**: Resources like food and water directly influence regional population happiness, which affects taxes and labor.
- Business maintenance: Running businesses will cost a small but consistent amount of resources, including water and food for the population, electricity for operations, and wood and steel for repairs and materials. These upkeep costs encourage players to manage resources efficiently over time.

---

This structured resource system enhances gameplay by fostering strategic decision-making, encouraging interaction, and driving competition.
